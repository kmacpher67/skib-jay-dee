# Perk Naming Notebook

**Created by:** Claude Sonnet 5 — 2026-07-27
**Purpose:** A running record of the creative/naming side of pickups and
perks — the jokes considered, the ones that landed, and why — kept
separate from the mechanical specs in `docs/roadmap.md` and the
handoffs. Mechanics live in the handoff plan; the *flavor* lives here so
it doesn't get lost or re-litigated in a future session.

This repo's pickups follow a consistent poop-pun naming convention: Rod
of Poopdom, Schleimy Potion, Broth Slip, Soggy Toilet Paper, Heavy
Plunger, Gawd Particle. New pickups should fit that voice.

## Turdstone Token (Resurrection Ward)

**Status:** Named, not yet built. See
[roadmap-handoff-v0.4.52-plan.md](handoffs/roadmap-handoff-v0.4.52-plan.md)
for the mechanical spec.

**The pitch:** a Call of Duty "Tombstone" perk homage — a rare pickup
that, once held, saves you from the full death penalty exactly once:
you keep your loadout and respawn on the same level instead of the
run's usual "death bumps you forward a level" behavior.

### Name options considered

- **Tombstone Perk** — the original working title Ken pasted in with
  the mechanical spec. Clear, but a straight CoD reference with no
  in-universe flavor.
- **Tombstone Poop** — Ken's own in-chat shorthand while discussing it;
  gets the pun in but reads a little flat next to the more specific
  Rod of Poopdom / Schleimy Potion style names.
- **The Turdstone Token** — Ken's pick. Keeps the "Tombstone" sound-alike
  (so the CoD reference still lands for anyone who knows it) while
  fully committing to the toilet-pun voice the rest of the item roster
  uses. "Token" also reads well as a single-use collectible, distinct
  from a "Rod" (weapon) or "Potion" (buff).

**Decision:** **The Turdstone Token**, in-chat shorthand "TurdPOOP
Perk." Internal pickup-type id: `'turdstone-token'`.

### Sprite concept

**The Holy Crap** — a classic CoD-style gray gravestone silhouette,
redrawn as a toilet (the cross/rounded-top gravestone shape reimagined
as a toilet tank-and-bowl silhouette, keeping the somber gray stone
coloring so it still reads as "gravestone" at a glance before the joke
lands). Not yet drawn/sourced — needs actual art before this ships; see
the open item in the plan handoff.
