# Yoodelling Unc Alex

**Name:** Yoodelling Unc Alex
**Vibe:** The yodeling uncle bit; an Alpine-adjacent gag and x-lax explosive diarriha touchdown celebration, he's a sore winner or looser.

### Parody Attributes
*   **Panic Index:** 6 (A casual, jaunty walking pace. He's in no rush, which is somehow scarier.)
*   **Grip Control:** 7 (Fairly steady, though he occasionally trips over his own Dutch Boy paint can.)
*   **Scream Volume:** 20 (Deafening yodels that bounce off the map walls and confuse the audio radar.)
*   **Sus Level:** 19 (Unbelievably out of place in a bathroom maze.)

### Character Details
*   **Main Scare:** Drops unexpectedly from the ceiling blowing a massive digital alphorn.
*   **Killing Tricks:** "The Yodel Echo" - Reverses your joystick controls momentarily while his yodel echoes through the maze, causing you to run directly into him.
*   **Best Dialogs:** 
    *   "RI-CO-LAAAAA!"
    *   "I'm telling your mother!"
    *   "Yodel-ay-hee-hoo, down the drain with you!"
    *   "Got my paint can, got my plunger!"
