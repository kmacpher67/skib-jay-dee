# Toiletman Wet

**Name:** Toiletman Wet Farm Grandpa 
**Vibe:** Drenched, straight out of the bowl. Mad as hell.

### Parody Attributes
* **Panic Index:** 18 (Flailing, thrashing, and rushing at terrifying speeds.)
* **Grip Control:** 1 (Zero friction. He over-slides past every corner, making his movements completely erratic.)
* **Scream Volume:** 12 (Wet, gurgling shrieks that sound like a backed-up drain.)
* **Sus Level:** 20 (Literally dripping with contaminated toilet water. Maximum suspicion.)

### Character Details
* **Main Scare:** Drops from the ceiling in a massive, screen-obscuring splash of highly questionable water.
* **Killing Tricks:** "The Wipeout" - Leaves a permanent, low-grip puddle trail wherever he slides. If the player steps in it, their Grip Control drops to 0 and they slide uncontrollably into the nearest wall (or directly into him).
* **Best Dialogs:**
	* "I NEED A TOWEL!"
	* "PLURGHH-GLUB-GLUB!"
	* "YOU LEFT THE SEAT UP!"
	* "CLEANUP ON AISLE TWO!"
