# Dad Case

**Name:** Dad Case
**Vibe:** Deadpan "dad energy" chaser.

### Parody Attributes
*   **Panic Index:** 2 (Slow, steady, and inevitable. He never runs, he just power-walks with purpose.)
*   **Grip Control:** 10 (Maximum traction. Those pristine white New Balance sneakers never slip on tile.)
*   **Scream Volume:** 15 (Booming, authoritative dad-voice that shakes the screen.)
*   **Sus Level:** 12 (He just looks highly disappointed in your life choices.)

### Character Details
*   **Main Scare:** Steps out from behind a bathroom stall holding a thermostat, shaking his head slowly.
*   **Killing Tricks:** "The Grounded" - Automatically triggers environmental traps that slam nearby doors shut and turn off the lights to "save electricity" as he approaches.
*   **Best Dialogs:**
    *   "Do you think we own the electric company?!"
    *   "I'm not mad, Jayden. I'm just disappointed."
    *   "Ask your mother."
    *   "Who left the lights on in here?!"
